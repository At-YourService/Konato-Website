We are looking for Analysts to join our team!   

Are you interested in a new challenge, alternating environments where you can broaden your knowledge? Then we have an opening in our team for you!

#### Challenges

As a Business / Functional analyst you will build a bridge between “the business” and IT.

- You will draft up the “ideal” solutions considering the information given by all stakeholders.

- You will document and specify solutions (use cases, information modelling, etc.)

- On a functional level it is your responsibility to support and guide the development teams.

- During the development process you will follow-up with the users, developers, architects, project managers, etc.

- You will support business acceptation tests.

- You are responsible for guarding the scope, ensuring quality, and following up on planning and deadlines.

#### Wishlist

- You have experience as a functional or business analyst.

- You have knowledge of UML, BPM, Agile, ICT, etc.

- You derive power from changing and challenging environments.

- Ambition and personal growth are your keywords.

- You are fluent in Dutch and English; French is a strong plus.

#### **What 's in for you**

- We provide cross-industry projects with a technology flavor.

- You will be guided by a “personal coach” and we will offer you a personal growth plan.

- You can spar with colleagues and your coach to discuss difficult projects.

- We will provide the necessary training, courses and certifications tailored to your personal interests.

- Konato-team-spirit: recurring team & knowledge sharing events, WhatsApp, yearly teambuilding weekend, afterwork drinks, etc.

- Competitive salary package including a car and the usual fringe benefits.
