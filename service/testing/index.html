Testing

We test your projects

We offer expertise in assuring software quality, in a traditional and agile context. Together with the customer, we apply a pragmatic approach and focus on the efficiency of testing.

  • Our consultants are certified professionals taking testing and quality as a key success factor for projects.
  • A test automation strategy can be designed together with the clients so the quality is assured during every software release.
  • During the functional testing we assure the delivered software is still aligned with the requirements and expected solution.
  • The non-functional testing will focus on performance, sanity, usability and compatibility to deliver a successful software product.

Konato is a co-pilot you can rely on to manage your projects. We are supportive and focused on getting the job done.
